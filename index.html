Know more about us!

Find answers to the most common questions about Order.uk

How does Order.uk work?

Choose a restaurant, add your favorite items to the cart and complete your order. You can then follow the delivery status in real time.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Cash payment is also available in selected areas.

Can I track my order in real-time?

Yes! Once your order is confirmed, you can track the delivery status live on our website or mobile app, from preparation to your doorstep.

Are there any special discounts or promotions available?

We regularly offer exclusive deals and discounts. Check our "Special Offers" section and subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated.

Is Order.uk available in my area?

We are expanding rapidly! Enter your postcode in the search box on the homepage to see if we deliver to your area.
